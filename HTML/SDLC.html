Software Developement life cycle

it is a structured framework that is used by software Egineering teams to plan ,build,test,deploy software efficiently.The Primary goal of SDLC is to miimize risks, ensure high quality ,lower cost,meet customer on schedule.
it comes in 6 phases
1.Planning:
    in this phase,figure out the software what needs to do.from stackholder,acccess feasibility(techinical,finiancial,time) scope of the projoect.
    2.design:
    translate requirements into a blueprint .software architechture,ui/ux mockups,
    3.implementation:
    in this phase,actual coding of the software takes place.using programming languages,frameworks,tools to build the software according to the design specifications.
    4.testing:in this phase,software is tested to identify and fix bugs,errors,or any issues that may affect its functionality,performance,or security.
    5.deployment:after successful testing,software is deployed to production environment for end-users
    6.maintenance:after deployment,software requires ongoing maintenance and support to address issues,apply updates,add new features,and ensure its continued functionality and performance.
